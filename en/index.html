About

A village pizzeria worth the detour

Pizzéria Žaba sits on the main street of Kostolište, a few minutes from Malacky. Locals come for lunch and evening pizza, others have it delivered home.

Reviews keep coming back to the same thing: a crust stuffed with cheese, topped with sesame and brushed with garlic. At lunchtime there is a daily menu alongside the regular offer.

How to order

Three ways, one phone number

  • Eat in

    Come to Hlavná 67 in Kostolište. There is a small car park by the door and more spaces across the street.

  • Takeaway

    Call ahead and pick up your pizza ready to go.

  • Delivery

    We deliver around Kostolište, to Malacky and nearby villages such as Gajary or Jakubov. Farther villages may have a minimum order, just ask when you call.

Guest tip: if you like your pizza extra crispy, say so when you order.
